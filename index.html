What do you want to build?

What’s new in 3.21.41

A new Charcoal look with four grayscale themes. Use your connected ChatGPT plan to create or edit icons and widescreen thumbnails from a reference. Without ChatGPT connected, Hybrid finds matching artwork in its expanded local library.

How to install

  1. Click Download and open Hybrid-Setup-3.21.41.exe.
  2. Windows may show a publisher warning because the installer is unsigned.
  3. Click Next until it finishes. A Hybrid shortcut appears on your desktop.
  4. Open Hybrid. Use Setup to connect Claude or ChatGPT for AI features.
